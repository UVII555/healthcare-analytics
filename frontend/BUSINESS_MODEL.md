# MedConnect — Business Model

## Target Customer
Small-to-mid size hospitals (50-500 beds) in Tier 2/3 Indian cities
who currently use paper/Excel for operations tracking.

## Revenue Model
SaaS subscription — 3 tiers (Starter/Growth/Enterprise)
Per-hospital pricing, not per-patient (predictable revenue)

## Key Metric for Investors
- MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue)
- Hospital retention rate
- Feature adoption rate (% hospitals using analytics vs just booking)
